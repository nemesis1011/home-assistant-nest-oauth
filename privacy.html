Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Purpose

This application connects my personal Google Nest devices to my self-hosted Home Assistant installation for home automation and monitoring.

Information Accessed

The application accesses Google Nest device information and controls authorized through Google's Smart Device Management API.

Data Storage and Use

Device information is processed by my personal Home Assistant installation for home automation and monitoring. The application does not operate a public service or sell personal information.

Data Sharing

The application does not independently share Nest device information with advertisers or other third parties. Home Assistant and Google's services operate according to their respective configurations and policies.

Access and Revocation

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account security settings. Locally stored integration data can be managed through Home Assistant.

Contact

This application is intended solely for personal use. Questions about this policy may be submitted through the project's GitHub repository.
